# Social Attention Research

This context defines the language used to record evidence and decisions while
studying learned mouse-trajectory representations.

## Language

**Research checkpoint**:
An immutable, date-indexed snapshot of the evidence, conclusions, limitations,
and next hypotheses accepted at one stage of the study.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, when it could be confused with saved model weights

**Supported finding**:
A conclusion directly backed by recorded evidence under a stated experiment
contract. It does not imply replication across training runs.
_Avoid_: Proven result, universal conclusion

**Inconclusive finding**:
An investigated claim that the recorded evidence cannot distinguish from no
difference. It remains unresolved rather than accepted or rejected.
_Avoid_: No effect, equivalent models

**Candidate intervention**:
A controlled change proposed for a future experiment but not yet supported by
evidence.
_Avoid_: Solution, improvement
